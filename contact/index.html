Contact Pamcoal

How to reach the editor for corrections, methodology questions, vendor updates, and operator interviews about call tracking for the trades.

Editorial corrections and vendor updates

If something on this site is wrong, send me an email. Subject line should include the URL of the page in question. I review corrections within two business days and post fixes the same day they are confirmed. Email editor@pamcoal.com.

Press and partnerships

For press inquiries, email press@pamcoal.com. I am not currently accepting sponsored content or paid placement requests. The rankings are based on the published methodology, period.

Methodology questions

If you want to replicate any of the testing, the test campaigns, landing pages, and per-platform setup logs are available on request. Email me with the page URL and the methodology question.

Operator interviews

I am always interested in talking to active contractor operators about their call tracking setup. If you run an HVAC, plumbing, roofing, or electrical shop with 2-plus trucks and you have an opinion on which platform is or is not working, I would like to hear it. Interviews are 30 minutes, on background by default, with quotes paraphrased and identifiers redacted.

No contact form on this page on purpose. Email is the fastest way to reach me.
